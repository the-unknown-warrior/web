Same website. Half the bill.

If your site lives on a subscription website builder, we'll rebuild it properly and host it ourselves — usually landing around half of what you're paying today.

How it works

We rebuild it, then we host it.

Send us a link to your current site. We rebuild the same pages on infrastructure we run ourselves — same content, same look if you want it kept, or a refresh if you don't — with no page-builder subscription sitting in the middle anymore.

Once the rebuilt version is live on our hosting, you cancel the old plan. Editing and managing the site day to day doesn't get harder — if anything, it gets faster, since it's no longer running inside someone else's platform.

What moves over

Everything your visitors already use, rebuilt properly

A migration covers the parts of the site people actually interact with, not just the pages.

D

Design

Your existing layout and branding, carried over as closely as you want — or refreshed, if you'd rather use the move as a chance to update it.

C

Content & pages

Every page, post and piece of copy on the current site gets carried across, checked, and put back in place.

F

Forms & bookings

Contact forms, booking widgets and anything else visitors submit are rebuilt to work the same way, or better.

Pricing

Roughly half of what you're paying now — worked out with you.

Every current site is different, so every quote is too. There's no calculator on this page for a reason.

As a rough guide: a site running around $20/month on a typical subscription website builder tends to land near $10/month once it's rebuilt and hosted with us — about half. That's a starting point for a conversation, not an automatic price. We'll look at your actual site, its traffic and what it needs before we agree on a number together.
