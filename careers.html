Ownership over instructions

You'll be trusted to run a real piece of a venture, not just execute a ticket. We hire people who want that.

Global by default

How hiring works

Four steps, start to offer

No surprise rounds. You'll know what's next after every conversation.

01

Apply

Send your details and a note on what you'd want to work on — through a role above or a general note to careers@glazni.com.

02

Intro call

A 30-minute conversation about your background and the venture you'd be joining.

03

Working session

A practical, paid conversation around real work — no throwaway take-homes.

04

Offer

If it's a fit on both sides, you'll have an offer within days, not weeks.
