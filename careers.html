GZ-102 Platform / DevOps Engineer Remote · Full-time

About the role

You'll build and run the infrastructure every Glazni Apps build ships on — from a developer's first local build to the cluster that serves it in production, and eventually the infra other ventures like Migrate lean on too.

What you'll do

  • Run and scale Kubernetes clusters, with Karpenter handling node autoscaling, across client and in-house workloads
  • Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines in Jenkins for Golang and Flutter builds
  • Keep local dev environments reproducible with Kind, so "works on my machine" stops being a real problem
  • Own core AWS infrastructure — EC2, S3, and the networking between them
  • Run monitoring and logging with Prometheus, Grafana and the EFK stack (Elasticsearch, Fluentd, Kibana) across every environment

What we're looking for

  • Production experience running Kubernetes at more than toy scale
  • Comfortable scripting infrastructure in Golang, not just writing YAML
  • Real AWS experience beyond the console — cost awareness, incident response
  • Bonus: Bazel or CMake experience from a past build-systems role
  • Hands-on experience with Prometheus/Grafana-based monitoring and centralized logging (EFK or ELK)

  • Type Full-time

How hiring works

Four steps, start to offer

No surprise rounds. You'll know what's next after every conversation.

01

Apply

Send your details and a note on what you'd want to work on — through a role above or a general note to careers@glazni.com.

02

Intro call

A 30-minute conversation about your background and the venture you'd be joining.

03

Working session

A practical, paid conversation around real work — no throwaway take-homes.

04

Offer

If it's a fit on both sides, you'll have an offer within days, not weeks.
