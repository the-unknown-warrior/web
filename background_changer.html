Glazni Apps — Background Changer
In development

A wallpaper that knows what time it is.

Background Changer swaps your wallpaper as the day moves — morning, evening, night — so your phone looks like itself at 8am and 8pm without you touching a setting. Add your own collection and it keeps circling through it, timed to the day.

The idea

Set it once, and your wallpaper does the rest.

Most phones show the same static wallpaper whether it's sunrise or midnight. Background Changer treats the wallpaper as something that should move with the day — brighter, warmer tones in the morning, something calmer once it's dark.

Add your own photos or pick from a built-in set, and the app keeps circling through them on its own schedule, tied to morning, evening and night — no manual switching, no third-party widget to babysit.

How it works

Three settings, then it runs itself

Build the collection once — the schedule takes it from there.

01

Add

Drop in your own wallpapers, or start from the built-in collection — as many or as few as you want in rotation.

02

Schedule

The app tags each wallpaper for morning, evening or night, and switches automatically as the time of day changes.

03

Circle

Inside each time slot, it keeps cycling through everything you've added, so it never looks the same two days running.
