About

From a single studio to an index of ventures.

Same craft and ownership standards, now spread across seven teams instead of one.

Glazni started as a mobile-first software studio in Moscow — an idea Aleksandr Gabushev first put together, with Franz Graaf joining not long after to help build it out — building fast, native-feeling Android and iOS apps, plus the websites, desktop tools, and infrastructure around them, for clients across Russia, Germany and the rest of Europe. As that studio grew, the teams around it — marketing, talent, hospitality tech, design, privacy-first computing, and website migration — grew into ventures of their own, each now run day to day by its own team. Glazni is now the holding company that sits above all of them: still rooted in Moscow, with Berlin as its European hub and remote coverage across the rest of the continent.

01

Independently run

Each venture owns its own roadmap and its own P&L. Nobody waits on a parent company's queue.

02

Shared backbone

Recruiting and people operations are built once — in Glazni Talent — and every venture in the index draws on the same hiring pipeline.

03

Same roots

Every venture is held to the craft and ownership standards the original Moscow studio was built on.

How it grows

Four steps from idea to venture.

The same path every venture in the index has taken so far.

01

Identify

We look for problems worth solving — usually ones a team inside Glazni has already run into first-hand.

02

Build

A small team ships a working product the way the original studio did: fast, and in production.

03

Operate

Once it's proven, the team runs it as its own venture — own name, own roadmap, own hiring.

04

Scale

The venture draws on Glazni Talent and the shared engineering backbone so it can grow without rebuilding the basics.
