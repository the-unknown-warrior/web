A blank page, once a day.

Journal is exactly what it sounds like — a plain text entry, dated automatically, with nothing else asked of you. No mood trackers, no prompts, no streak counter guilting you into writing. Just a place to put down what happened, or what you're thinking, whenever you feel like it.

The idea

Not everything needs a feature.

Most journaling apps compete on prompts, mood graphs and streaks — extra layers between you and just writing something down. Journal skips all of it. Open the app, there's today's blank entry, and you write in it or you don't.

Entries are plain text, sorted by date, nothing more. Miss a day and there's no broken streak to feel bad about — just an empty day sitting in the timeline, and tomorrow's entry waiting underneath it.

How it works

Three things, that's the whole app

No settings to configure before you can start writing.

01

Write

Open the app to today's entry and start typing. It saves as you go — there's no save button to remember.

02

Browse

Every past entry lines up by date underneath, plain text, easy to skim or search back through.

03

Keep

Export the whole journal as a plain text backup any time — it's yours, not locked to the app.
