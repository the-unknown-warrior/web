In development

Turn any PDF into something you can listen to.

Drop in a PDF, get back an audiobook. It's free if you can wait — conversions queue up and can take up to about a week depending on demand. Pay, and yours jumps the line and comes back instantly. Either way, your document is only ever used to make your audio and is deleted afterward — not stored, not scanned for anything else.

The idea

Books don't need an account to become audio.

PDF to Audiobook does exactly what it sounds like — upload a document, get back a finished audio file. No reading app to switch to, no library account, no subscription you forgot you had. The conversion itself works the same whichever way you go; the only difference is where you sit in the queue.

This is one of the few Glazni Apps projects that mostly runs as a paid service, because the compute to convert a document into natural-sounding narration isn't free — but it's never locked behind a subscription. Pay per book if you want it now, or don't pay at all and it still arrives, just later.

Pricing

Free if you can wait. Instant if you can't.

The app itself is a free download. Each conversion is either queued for free or paid to skip the line.

Free
Wait for it
  • No cost, ever — no card required
  • Joins a shared conversion queue
  • Turnaround of up to about a week, depending on demand
  • Same narration quality as paid
  • Document deleted once the audio is ready
Paid
Instant
  • Pay per book, no subscription forced
  • Skips straight to the front of the queue
  • Ready in minutes, not days
  • Same narration quality as free
  • Document deleted once the audio is ready
How it works

Three steps, either way you go

Upload once — the only choice you're making is how long you're willing to wait.

01

Upload

Drop in a PDF and the app pulls out the readable text — works best on text-based documents; heavily scanned pages may need OCR first.

02

Convert

Choose free and it joins the queue — up to about a week depending on demand — or pay and it's processed right away, usually in minutes.

03

Listen

Download the finished audio file straight to your device. It's yours to keep — no app lock-in, no streaming required.
