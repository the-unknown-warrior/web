Glazni Apps — Contacts Exporter
In development

Every contact, out of Google and into a file you actually own.

Contacts Exporter does one job: read every contact stored on your phone and turn it into a plain CSV file, saved locally. No account to create, no cloud sync to trust, nothing left behind in someone else's database. Next time you switch phones — or ecosystems entirely — your contacts move with you, not with a login.

The idea

Your address book shouldn't only exist inside someone else's account.

For most people, "backing up contacts" quietly means "trusting Google to keep them forever." That's fine right up until you want to leave — a new phone, a new operating system, a decision to de-Google — and discover your own address book was never really a file you could just take with you.

Contacts Exporter fixes that by producing the most boring, portable format there is: a CSV. Names, numbers, emails — one row per contact, readable by literally anything, forever. It's not trying to replace Google Contacts. It's just making sure you don't need it to.

How it works

Three steps, no account required

The app never phones home with your address book — the whole point is that it doesn't have to.

01

Read

With your permission, the app reads the contacts already stored on your phone — nothing added, nothing changed.

02

Convert

Every contact becomes a row in a clean CSV file: name, numbers, emails and the rest, laid out plainly.

03

Keep

Save the file locally, back it up wherever you like, or import it straight into your next phone — with or without Google in the middle.
