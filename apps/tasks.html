A short list, not a system.

Tasks is a plain to-do list — add what needs doing, check it off, done. No projects, no labels, no productivity system to maintain, just today's list and whatever's still open from before.

The idea

The list is the whole app.

Most task apps grow into project managers — boards, tags, recurring rules, reminders stacked on reminders. Tasks stays a list: add an item, check it off when it's done, delete it if it isn't happening.

Nothing rolls over automatically and nothing nags you. What's left at the end of the day is still there tomorrow, exactly where you left it.

How it works

Three things, that's the whole app

Nothing to configure before you can start adding to the list.

01

Add

Type a task and it's on the list — no due date, priority or project to assign unless you want to write it into the text.

02

Check

Tap to mark something done. It stays visible, struck through, until you clear it.

03

Clear

Clear finished tasks whenever you want a fresh list — nothing disappears on its own.
