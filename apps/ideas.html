The idea

Most note apps make you organize before you've thought anything yet.

The usual note app asks you to pick a notebook, a folder or a tag before you've even written the thought down — so the good ones get lost hunting for the right place to put them. Ideas removes that step. You create one idea, once, as a single heading. Every note you add after that — a line, a link, a half-formed thought at 2am — drops straight underneath it, in order, with nothing else to decide.

There's no second level. Notes don't get their own sub-notes, and ideas don't nest inside other ideas. If a thought belongs to an idea, it goes under that idea's heading and stays there — a running page per idea instead of a tree you have to maintain.

How it works

Start it, feed it, find it again

The whole app is three steps, and the third one is just scrolling.

01

Start an idea

Give it a name and nothing else. That name becomes the one subheading everything about it will live under.

02

Drop in notes

Whenever a thought about it shows up, open the idea and add it as a note. No new screen, no picking a folder — it just gets added to the pile.

03

Come back to it

Open the idea any time and every note is right there, in the order you thought of it — the whole shape of the idea at a glance.
