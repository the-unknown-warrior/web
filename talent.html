Venture 03 — Glazni Talent

Good hires, without paying premium-market rates.

Glazni Talent runs recruiting and people operations for every venture in the index — and for outside companies who want the same access to skilled hires across global talent markets, at a fraction of the cost of hiring locally.

How it works

We source, vet and manage the hire — you get the output.

We built Glazni Talent to solve our own hiring first: sourcing skilled engineers, marketers and operators from emerging markets, at a cost far below hiring the same skill set locally. Once the model worked for us, we opened it up.

We handle sourcing, screening and ongoing people operations. You get a vetted hire who reports into your team, without carrying the overhead of a full local hiring process.
