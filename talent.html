Venture 03 — Glazni Talent

Build a team abroad, without opening an entity yourself.

Glazni Talent runs recruiting and people operations for every venture in the index — and for EU companies who want to hire in global talent markets like India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nigeria and other places without the overhead of a local entity. We work as your Employer of Record, help you stand up your own branch office, or simply source and vet the hire — so you get skilled talent at a fraction of the cost of hiring locally.

Talk to us about hiring See our services
How it works

We source, vet and manage the hire — you choose the structure.

We built Glazni Talent to solve our own hiring first: sourcing skilled engineers, marketers and operators from emerging markets — India, Pakistan, Bangladesh and Nigeria chief among them — at a cost far below hiring the same skill set locally. Once the model worked for us, we opened it up.

From there you choose how deep you want to go: hire through us as an Employer of Record with no entity required, bring on a sourced and vetted hire directly, or let us help you set up and staff your own branch office if you're ready to put down roots in-market.
