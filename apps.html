Venture 01 — Glazni Apps

Where Glazni started, and where most of it still runs.

Before there was a holding company, there was a small studio in Moscow building fast, native-feeling iOS and Android apps. That's still what Glazni Apps does — for clients, and occasionally for ourselves.

Talk to us about a build See open roles
What we build

Native apps, built the way the original studio built them.

The team behind Glazni Apps ships production iOS and Android software on Flutter, Golang and AWS — the same stack the studio was founded on. A number of these apps are live on the App Store and Google Play today, and several more are in active development.

Most of what we build is commissioned work: client products we design, ship and maintain under our own name or theirs. What's public and what isn't is a decision each client makes, not us.

Private, by design

We don't list or link to our own apps here.

If you already have a link to one of our apps, that link works. This site just won't hand it to you.

Why: most of what we build is client work, and several of our own apps are still early or invite-based. Rather than publish a partial, half-public list, we simply don't route traffic to any Glazni-built app from this website. If you were sent a Play Store or App Store link directly, it's valid — go ahead and open it. Nothing here will redirect you to one.
Also available

Already have a product? We'll rebuild it properly.

Not every engagement starts from a blank page. A good share of our client work is re-engineering: taking an existing website, app or internal tool that's grown slow, brittle or outdated, and rebuilding it on a stack that can actually be maintained.

If that's what you need, this is the venture to talk to — same team, same standards, applied to something that already exists.
