Mobile applications

No briefs, no NDAs, no one paying us to build these. We pick a small problem we actually have, build a focused, privacy-first app to solve it, put it on the store, and try to make it work as a real product — on its own.

What we build

Premium, privacy-first apps — built for one job each.

Every app on this page exists because someone on the team wanted it. Tech Stack includes — Flutter, Golang, HTMX and AWS — along with a few other things as necessary. Every project is different: pick one job, do it well, keep it private and simple by default.

We fund and ship these ourselves, on our own timeline. Some will stay free, some may carry a small one-time price or a light subscription — whatever makes sense for that app to keep existing.

NOTE: If what you actually want is something built to your spec, that's a different venture: see Glazni Studio.

Private, by design

We don't list or link to our own apps here — yet.

If you already have a link to one of our apps, that link works. This site just won't hand it to you.

Why: most of what's below is still early, invite-based, or mid-build. Rather than publish a partial, half-public list, we simply don't route traffic to any Glazni app from this website. If you were sent a Play Store or App Store link directly, it's valid — go ahead and open it. Nothing here will redirect you to one.
