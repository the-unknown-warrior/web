GrapheneOS guides

Comprehensive, step-by-step, kept up to date.

We write and maintain in-depth guides for installing and living on GrapheneOS — from flashing your first Pixel to sandboxing Google Play services, choosing alternative app stores, and hardening the OS beyond its defaults.

No sponsored placements, no affiliate detours. Just the steps, written by people who run this setup daily.

De-googled phones

We flash them, harden them, and ship them ready to use.

We source supported handsets, install GrapheneOS ourselves, strip Google services out, and set up sane defaults before the phone ever reaches you — no setup guide required on your end, though we'll happily walk you through it.

Available models depend on current stock. Get in touch and we'll confirm what's ready to ship.
